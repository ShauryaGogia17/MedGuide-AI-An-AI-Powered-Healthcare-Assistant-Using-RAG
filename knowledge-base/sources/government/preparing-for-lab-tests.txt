Preparing for laboratory tests — educational summary of MedlinePlus (U.S. National Library of Medicine).
Preparation requirements depend on the test. Ask the ordering healthcare professional which instructions apply. Food, activity, medicines, and supplements can affect some results. Tell the healthcare team about medicines and supplements and whether you followed the preparation instructions. Do not stop prescribed medicines without discussing it with the healthcare professional. If instructions are unclear or were not followed, contact the healthcare team before the test.
Source: https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/how-to-prepare-for-a-lab-test/
